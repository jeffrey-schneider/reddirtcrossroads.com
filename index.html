Stories of Oklahoma, consequence, and second chances

Some roads take you home.
Others change who you are before you get there.

Red Dirt Crossroads is a growing collection of stories about people whose lives turn at unexpected intersections—through choices, consequences, loss, friendship, and the possibility of beginning again.

Featured Series

The Stephanie Wilburn-Hammond Series

Stephanie's story follows a life overturned by the criminal justice system and the people whose lives become intertwined with hers along the way.

Red Dirt Crossroads collects material from that world: stories, characters, places, photographs, and other pieces that extend beyond the pages of the books.
